# Posadas Retail Sales & Inventory Analytics

A hands-on business analytics project based on a small retail and hardware business model in Posadas, Misiones, Argentina.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales, revenue, product performance, inventory, pricing, and gross margins using a realistic retail dataset.

Product prices are based on publicly available price references from Posadas, Misiones, and comparable Argentine retail and construction-material sources. Transaction data is simulated for analytical purposes and does not represent the actual sales or inventory of any specific business.

## Business Questions

- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which products have the highest sales volume?
- How does revenue change over time?
- Which products generate the highest gross margins?
- Which products may require inventory attention?
- How do online and in-store sales compare?
- How can sales and inventory data support better business decisions?

## Data

The dataset includes:

- Date
- Order ID
- Product
- Category
- Units Sold
- Unit Price
- Revenue
- Estimated Cost
- Gross Profit
- Gross Margin
- Sales Channel
- Inventory Level

## Disclaimer

This is a portfolio project created for demonstration and learning purposes. Sales transactions and business performance data are simulated, while product and price references are based on publicly available market information.
